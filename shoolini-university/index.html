Shoolini University is one of the fifty-plus organisations we have worked with since 2023, and sits in the education and training group on our client wall.

Universities sit on both sides of our business at once: they are employers with their own HR to run, and they are where the next intake of talent comes from. Our campus programme, RecruitXcel, exists because of partnerships like this one.

What usually needs fixing in this sector

Across the organisations we work with in education, the same handful of problems keep coming up. They are worth naming, because most owners assume theirs is unique:

  • Two very different workforces under one roof. Faculty and administrative staff need different hiring, appraisal and progression rules, and a single HR policy serves neither well.
  • Hiring is seasonal and then urgent. Faculty recruitment concentrates around the academic calendar, so the same work has to be done in a fraction of the year.
  • Appraisals have to withstand scrutiny. Academic promotion criteria are formal, which means the evidence behind every rating has to exist and be findable.
  • Placement outcomes are the product. How well students are placed drives the next intake, so employability training is not a side activity.

Where we help

  • Recruitment for faculty, administrative and operations roles, with background checks.
  • RecruitXcel and campus programmes — employability skills, CV and interview preparation for students.
  • Evidence-linked appraisals through Alvora HRMS, so a rating can always be traced to the document behind it.
  • Alvora Learning — staff training delivered and tracked against one skills list.

Working with us

Client engagements run through the five steps of our Growth Loop — see what is true, shape the system, source the right people, strengthen them, and sustain it with evidence. Which steps apply depends entirely on what a health check finds, and we start there rather than arriving with a fixed answer.

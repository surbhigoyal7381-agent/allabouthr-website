What usually needs fixing in this sector

Across the organisations we work with in manufacturing and industrial, the same handful of problems keep coming up. They are worth naming, because most owners assume theirs is unique:

  • Shop floor and office run on different rules. Attendance, overtime and leave are handled one way in the plant and another in the office, and nobody can produce one number for the group.
  • Statutory exposure across multiple units. EPF, ESIC, contract labour and POSH obligations multiply with every unit and every contractor.
  • Contractor workforce invisible to HR. A large share of the people on site are not on the payroll, so their papers, compliance and timesheets live in a different world.
  • Appraisals that cannot be defended. With long-service staff and family-run decision making, increment season becomes a negotiation rather than a process.

Where we help

  • Recruitment — accounts, finance, engineering and plant roles through search and bulk hiring, with background checks.
  • Compliance you can prove — a record you could hand straight to an inspector, plus POSH end to end.
  • Alvora HRMS — one system for records, leave, attendance, shifts and payroll across units.
  • Alvora Gig — joining papers, compliance documents, timesheets and payments for contractor and vendor staff.

Working with us

Client engagements run through the five steps of our Growth Loop — see what is true, shape the system, source the right people, strengthen them, and sustain it with evidence. Which steps apply depends entirely on what a health check finds, and we start there rather than arriving with a fixed answer.

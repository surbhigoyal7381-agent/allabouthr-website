What usually needs fixing in this sector

Across the organisations we work with in manufacturing and engineering, the same handful of problems keep coming up. They are worth naming, because most owners assume theirs is unique:

  • Skilled trades are hard to replace. Fabricators, welders, quality inspectors and maintenance engineers take months to find and days to lose to a competitor paying a little more.
  • Nobody knows the real cost per job. Labour, rework and overtime are reconstructed at month-end, by which time it is far too late to change anything.
  • Supervisors were promoted for technical skill. The best fabricator becomes the supervisor, and then nobody develops them as a manager.
  • Training happens but is never measured. Safety and skills training is delivered and signed for, and no record connects it to whether anyone got better.

Where we help

  • Recruitment — engineering, quality, production and plant HR roles via search and bulk hiring.
  • Job levels and honest rating scales so two supervisors score the same person the same way.
  • Manager development for supervisors promoted on technical strength.
  • Kinexus Systems — a Baseline that counts the hours lost re-typing information between the shop floor and the office.

Working with us

Client engagements run through the five steps of our Growth Loop — see what is true, shape the system, source the right people, strengthen them, and sustain it with evidence. Which steps apply depends entirely on what a health check finds, and we start there rather than arriving with a fixed answer.

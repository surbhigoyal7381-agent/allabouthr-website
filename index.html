AllAboutHR · Strategic HR consulting

Fix the people problem. Then prove it stayed fixed.

We call it the Growth Loop. Five steps that take you from “something is wrong” to “here is the proof it improved”. Most HR firms hand you a report and leave. The last step of ours is checking the numbers a year later.

Two things sit alongside all five steps. Managed means we run the HR work for you instead of just advising — part-time or fully outsourced. Build is Kinexus: when a step needs software you don’t have, we build it rather than leaving you stuck.

Where AI sits, and where it does not

AI helps. A person decides.

What it does

  • Writes a first draft of a job advert you then edit
  • Points out a vague goal before a manager approves it
  • Answers “how much leave do I have left?” from your own policy, with the source
  • Explains a number in plain words — the maths is done by code, not by AI

What it never does

  • Decide anyone’s rating. The number that affects pay is chosen by a named person
  • Read faces, voices or moods. We have not built it and cannot add it later
  • Watch how people work and use it against them
  • Calculate leave balances or pay. That stays ordinary, checkable code

Every AI feature can be switched off, and everything still works without it. You are never stuck if the AI is wrong or unavailable.

AI is not the answer to everything

We only use AI where we can show you the hours it saves. If a simple rule or a better-designed form would do the same job for less money, we will build that instead — even though the AI version would earn us more.

We show you, before you pay

Before you commit to anything, we build a small working version using your own data. You judge the real thing, not a slide about it.

We are there with you at every step

About

We started in HR. Then we started building the software.

We have worked with Indian businesses since 2023. Alvora and Kinexus exist because the same thing kept happening — we would give good advice, and there was no system in the business able to carry it out.

Our reports kept telling owners things they could not act on. The findings were right. The problem was that nothing in the business recorded the thing that needed to change. A neat appraisal framework designed in a workshop does not survive in a company that still tracks attendance on a shared spreadsheet.

So we built the software ourselves. And then we built the layer underneath it, for businesses whose real problem was that they had no system at all. That is the whole story — and it is why the three parts of the company work together instead of competing for the same client.
